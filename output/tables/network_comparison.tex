The variable cost of a trip is the route distance multiplied by the ATRI marginal operating cost per mile, which reflects 2022 operating costs, of the technology in the origin state's region (Subsection~\ref{subsec:cfl_scenarios}), and the saving is the diesel cost less the electric or autonomous electric cost. Detour ratios and savings are averaged over pairs. In Panel C the savings are negative: the charging-feasible route would raise the variable cost of the trip, and these pairs remain on diesel.
